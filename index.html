Professional Introduction

Hello, I'm Andy Diaz Gomez a Registered Nurse from the beautiful city of Miami, Florida. I am looking for positions that will allow me to apply my understanding, hands-on training, and gentle manner in patient care in the areas of medical-surgical, emergency, and ICU, where there is a need for good teamwork, communication, and clinical decision-making skills.

My reason for deciding to become a nurse is the deep and steady bond that nurses have with patients. Such close contact enables nurses to detect even the slightest changes in the condition, initiating timely interventions, and preventing the occurrence of complications through careful and responsive care.

Among my qualities are good listening skills, staying composed in difficult situations, and the ability to communicate effectively. I strongly believe that educating patients effectively is one of the ways to empower them, and I do this by translating complicated health information into simple terms so that the patients can become active participants in their care and can make good health decisions.

During my clinical practice, I have worked with Behavioral Health at Evolutions Treatment Center and Medical-Surgical at Select Medical. This exposure has allowed me to build skills such as therapeutic communication, trust-building, and offering safe, well-organized, and patient-centered care. I base my actions on the principles of respect equity integrity, and service whenever I interact with patients and collaborate with other professionals.

Nursing Philosophy

Nursing, in my opinion, is professional care that uses evidence, clinical judgment, and compassion to uphold human dignity and assist people in healing, adapting, or living well with their illness. Being a bilingual student of nursing, I have witnessed that outcomes improve significantly when patients understand the plan and feel comfortable asking questions.

Nurses often know patients best because they spend sustained time with them during daily shifts. Much of nursing involves continuous observation, analysis, and timely intervention when a patient’s condition changes.

My core beliefs are respect, equity, integrity, and service. Respect is about being attentive and tailoring care to a patient's culture and language. Equity involves recognizing barriers—such as cost, stigma, or low health literacy—and finding sustainable solutions. Integrity is about recognizing boundaries and being truthful. I strive to be the nurse who detects changes early, communicates effectively, and raises concerns promptly when needed.
